# Contributing to Narek

Thank you for your interest. Corrections are welcome — especially to the text itself. Typos, misread characters, wrong punctuation, and misaligned sections are the most valuable thing you can send.

- Any rights you hold in repository-authored mappings, segmentation, arrangement, database structure, and metadata under `book/` are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0 Universal](./LICENSE-CC0). This does not alter the status of the public-domain original or third-party materials identified in [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES](./THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES).
- Contributions to third-party materials must comply with their applicable terms.
- All other original contributions are licensed under the [MIT License](./LICENSE-MIT).
- You wrote the contribution yourself or have the right to submit it under the applicable terms.

If you are not able to agree to that, open an issue describing the problem instead of a pull request. A precise bug report is worth as much as a patch here.

### Provenance

Do not submit third-party text unless it is in the public domain or you have the right to submit it under the applicable terms. Cite the edition and page for each textual correction; uncited emendations cannot be accepted.

See [THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES](./THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES) for third-party materials already present in this repository.
